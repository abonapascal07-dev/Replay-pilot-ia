Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ReplyPilot AI is an AI-powered customer support assistant for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information such as email addresses, business names, and business types. We also process customer support messages submitted by users to generate AI responses.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, provide AI-generated customer support drafts, maintain the service, troubleshoot problems, and protect against misuse.

3. AI Processing

Messages submitted for AI assistance are processed using third-party AI technology. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information.

4. Service Providers

We use third-party providers for authentication, hosting, AI processing, and payment services. These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services.

5. Payment Information

Subscription payments, when enabled, are processed by our designated payment provider. We do not intend to store complete payment card details on our application servers.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us to submit a privacy request.

9. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Applicable legal safeguards will be considered where required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The latest version will be published here.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, please use the support contact information published on the ReplyPilot AI website.
